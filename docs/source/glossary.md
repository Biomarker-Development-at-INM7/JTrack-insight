# Glossary

## Analysis Scope

The subset of dataset files or rows selected for a computation step.

## Feature Table

A table where rows represent a subject and time bin, and columns represent
derived measures such as steps, mobility, or heart-rate summaries.

## Raw Review

Visual inspection of original or near-original sensor values before scientific
interpretation of derived features.

## Temporal Frequency

The aggregation unit used for features, such as hourly, daily, monthly, or full
study duration.

## Wear State

A label indicating whether a wearable device was likely on wrist, off wrist, or
unknown during a measurement period.
